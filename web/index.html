Headfit

Pin a photo, get your head in 3D. Everything happens in this tab, nothing is uploaded, and no AI is involved: the head is bent to your pins by geometry.

Choose a photo of a face looking straight at the camera, or try a sample.

Level with the face, mouth closed, hair off the forehead works best.

Head mesh and expressions: ICT Face Model Light, © 2020 USC Institute for Creative Technologies, MIT licence. Sample faces are made up: the same head bent and painted by this project, not photos of anyone. Viewer: three.js, MIT licence. Font: Inter Tight, SIL Open Font Licence.
